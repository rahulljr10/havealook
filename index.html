About the Founder
I spent years inside the machine that EXPLOITS Indian brands. Now I'm building the exit.

I'm Rahul Khatri — a digital marketing professional who has watched, from the inside, how Meta and Google's rising ad costs chip away at the margins of genuinely good Indian brands.

After 2.5 years managing SEO, performance campaigns, and retention strategies for multiple brands — I stopped running ads for platforms that don't care about the brands depending on them.

Product Architecture
Product 01
LIVE
SESSIONS
Brands go live directly from their LIVELY profile. Three distinct modes — Sell Live, Connect Live, Launch Live — each scored on the right metrics, not vanity numbers.
  • Product pinning + one-tap buy
  • Real-time Q&A and objection handling
  • Remind Me for warm pre-live audience
  • Category-based discovery rows
Product 02
DROPS
Short-form 15–45s shoppable videos. Product tagging mandatory, not optional. Algorithm rewards purchase intent — taps, completions, orders — not dopamine engagement.
  • Full-screen vertical, one Drop per screen
  • No likes, comments, or vanity metrics
  • Commerce-first algorithm
  • One product card per Drop with Buy CTA
